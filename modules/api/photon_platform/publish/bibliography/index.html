photon_platform.publish.bibliography¶

Scholarly Bibliography formatting (Chicago, MLA, APA, BibTeX) and automated bidirectional passage backlinks (.. citation-formats:: and .. backlinks::).

Works directly from unified Card objects (local or inherited via :card_ref:) and Sphinx’s :cite:, :person:, and :term: inline role occurrences, grouping parallel companion tracks (e.g., LA · EN, EL · EN) automatically.
